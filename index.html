    IEC 61508 Safety Lifecycle

    Overall safety lifecycle (Fig. 2) with Box 10 realisation expanded into the E/E/PE system (Fig. 3) and software (Fig. 4) safety lifecycles, related as in Fig. 5.

    Analysis Overall planning Realisation / E/E/PE system Software Installation, operation & disposal
    Applies to all phasesVerification, management of functional safety and functional safety assessment are not drawn, but they are relevant to every overall, E/E/PE system and software lifecycle phase.
    Box 11Other risk reduction measures sit outside the scope of IEC 61508.
    Box 10 and the other partsIEC 61508-2 (hardware) and IEC 61508-3 (software) cover Box 10, and also the programmable electronic aspects of Boxes 13, 14 and 15 where relevant.
    One lifecycle per systemEach E/E/PE safety-related system has its own E/E/PE system safety lifecycle; its software lifecycle interacts with it in both directions.
    Subsequent phasesThe complete E/E/PE system and software lifecycles also contain system-specific instances of Boxes 12 to 16; only the realisation phases are expanded here.
    Operation & modification dataTechnical requirements for operation, maintenance, repair, modification, retrofit and decommissioning come from the supplier of the E/E/PE safety-related system.
    Objectives per phaseTable 1 (clause 7.1.2) gives each phase's objectives, scope, requirement subclause, required inputs and outputs — a good target for the step hyperlinks.
